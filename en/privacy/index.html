No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies. It stores nothing on your device and reads nothing from it. Your browsing behaviour is not analysed. No tracking services, no advertising networks, no maps and no social media plug-ins are embedded.

The fonts used are hosted on this website's own server. They are not loaded from an external provider. Opening a page establishes no connection to any third party. The only moment at which data leaves this website is when you submit the contact form.

Because no information is stored on or read from your device, consent under section 25 TDDDG is not required. That is also why you will find no cookie banner here.

Server log files

This website is hosted by Netlify, Inc., San Francisco, California, USA. Netlify acts as our processor under Art. 28 GDPR.

When a page is opened, data transmitted by your browser is collected automatically: IP address, date and time of access, the address requested, the page previously visited, and details of your browser and operating system.

The purpose is to deliver the website, to keep it running reliably and to defend against attacks. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest lies in the secure and uninterrupted operation of this website.

Contact form

The form is operated through Formspree, Inc., USA. Formspree acts as our processor under Art. 28 GDPR.

Only the fields you fill in are transmitted: name, company, email address, the service you select and your message. For technical reasons Formspree also receives your IP address and your browser details. Transmission takes place only when you click “Send message”. Before that, nothing leaves your browser.

The purpose is to handle your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR where your enquiry is directed at entering into or preparing a contract. In all other cases it is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, with our legitimate interest in being able to answer enquiries.
